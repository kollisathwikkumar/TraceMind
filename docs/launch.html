04 / HOW AN INVESTIGATION MOVES

From page
to next step.

Follow a deliberate response loop. Select a phase to see the work that belongs there; expand a step for the detail.

  1. Bring the alert, affected services, and first symptoms into one working view. Start from the incident as it was reported; preserve the original signal as the investigation evolves.

    INCIDENT CONTEXTAFFECTED SERVICESINITIAL SYMPTOMS

  2. Generate plausible causes from symptoms, service context, and known operational history. Keep more than one explanation alive until evidence strengthens or weakens it.

    HYPOTHESESCONFIDENCEALTERNATIVES

  3. Ask focused questions across the tools available to your environment. Compare the results, follow strong signals deeper, and keep source details attached to findings.

    TELEMETRYDEPENDENCIESRUNBOOKS

  4. Summarize the most likely cause with its supporting evidence, remaining unknowns, and a proposed remediation path that responders can review.

07 / GOOD TO KNOW

On your mind.

A few quick answers about the TraceMind approach.

Read the documentation ↗
What is TraceMind? +

TraceMind is an incident response workspace designed to help teams investigate operational issues with hypotheses, evidence, service context, and runbook-aware guidance.

Does it make changes to production? +

Investigation and remediation planning are distinct. Proposed steps can be reviewed, and execution behavior depends on the configured workflow and tools.

What systems can it connect to? +

Current project integrations include AWS and Kubernetes tooling, incident sources such as PagerDuty and OpsGenie, Slack workflows, and knowledge sources. Availability depends on configuration.

Can I try it without connecting production systems? +

Yes. The guided terminal demo runs a simulated investigation and does not require provider credentials.
